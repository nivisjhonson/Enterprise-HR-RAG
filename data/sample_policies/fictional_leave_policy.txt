FICTIONAL SAMPLE POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

Annual leave
Employees may request annual leave through the fictional People Portal. Requests
should be submitted to the employee's manager before the planned leave dates.
Approval depends on staffing and the manager's confirmation. This example does
not specify an annual allowance; employees should consult Human Resources for
their actual entitlement.

Sick leave
Employees who cannot work because of illness should notify their manager as soon
as reasonably possible and follow the organization's absence reporting process.
Contact Human Resources for documentation requirements and eligibility details.
